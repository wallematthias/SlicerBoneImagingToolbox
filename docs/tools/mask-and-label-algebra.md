# Mask and Label Algebra

Mask and Label Algebra creates derived masks and label maps from loaded Slicer segmentations or labelmaps. It is useful for preparing analysis ROIs, material labels, and combined compartments after contouring.

## Typical Uses

- Combine trabecular and cortical masks into a full analysis ROI.
- Intersect a bone segmentation with full, trabecular, cortical, or custom ROI masks.
- Build material label maps for FEA workflows.
- Rename or organize labels before exporting derivative artifacts.
